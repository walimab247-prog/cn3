Privacy Policy

What data we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 2026-08-14

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Crypto Coins News ("we", "us") handles information when you visit this website or contact us. Questions about this policy can be sent to info@cryptocoinnews.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact form data: when you write to us we receive the name, email address and message you provide. We use it only to respond to your inquiry.
  • Technical data: like most websites, our hosting infrastructure may log IP addresses, browser type and pages requested for security and reliability purposes.
  • Cookies: see our Cookie Policy for details on the limited cookies this site uses.

3. How we use information

We use the information described above to operate the website, respond to messages, protect the service from abuse, and understand aggregate readership patterns. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for automated decision-making that affects you.

4. Sharing

We share data only with service providers that host or protect this website (acting on our instructions), or where required by law. We do not share reader data with advertisers.

5. Retention

Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to handle the inquiry and any follow-up. Technical logs are retained for a limited period consistent with security practice and then deleted or anonymized.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights, email info@cryptocoinnews.com and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
