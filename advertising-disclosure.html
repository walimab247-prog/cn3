Advertising Disclosure

How advertising and sponsored content are handled on this site.

Last updated: 2026-08-14

1. Separation of editorial and advertising

Editorial content on Crypto Coins News is produced independently of any commercial relationship. Advertisers have no influence over what we cover, how we cover it, or the conclusions our analysis reaches.

2. Labeling

Any paid placement on this site is clearly marked with an "Advertisement" or "Sponsored" label. If content is not labeled, it is editorial content and nobody paid for it to appear. We do not publish native advertising disguised as news.

3. Affiliate links

If an article ever contains affiliate links (links that may earn us a commission), this is disclosed at the top of that article. As of the last update of this page, our news and market articles do not contain affiliate links.

4. What advertisers cannot buy

  • Favorable coverage or the removal of unfavorable coverage.
  • Placement of promises of profit or price predictions.
  • Editorial endorsement of any token, product or service.

5. Contact

For advertising inquiries, email ads@cryptocoinnews.com or use the contact form. All placements are subject to this disclosure policy.
