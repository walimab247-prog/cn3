Editorial Policy

How Crypto Coins News selects, produces and maintains its content.

Last updated: 2026-08-14

1. Purpose and independence

Crypto Coins News (CCN) publishes neutral, informational coverage of cryptocurrency markets. Our editorial decisions are made independently of any exchange, token project or advertiser. No third party can pay to influence the substance or tone of our news coverage.

2. Sourcing

Our articles are based on reporting and data from established crypto media and public sources, including CryptoSlate, CoinBureau, DailyCoin, Coin Edition, Coindoo, Coinpaper, CoinIDOL, CoinNews, CoinsProbe and CoinsCapture. We do not copy source material: every story is an original rewrite with added context and analysis, and every article lists its sources with links so readers can verify claims themselves.

3. What we will not publish

  • Promises of profit, "guaranteed returns" or any language implying certain investment outcomes.
  • Aggressive calls to buy, sell or hold any token.
  • Fabricated quotes, invented experts or unverifiable claims presented as fact.
  • Undisclosed sponsored content.

4. Attribution and transparency

Each article displays its publication date, its last update date and its authorship (individual author or the CCN Editorial Team). Where our information comes from a specific outlet, we say so and link to it.

5. Corrections and updates

Cryptocurrency markets move quickly. When material facts change, we update the article and refresh its "Updated" date. Substantive corrections are made visibly rather than silently. Readers can report errors via the contact page; the editorial team reviews every report.

6. Disclaimers

All content on this site is informational. Every market-related article carries a disclaimer that it is not financial advice. See the Risk Disclaimer for details.
