Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about navigating the market with us.

How often are the market metrics updated?

Headline metrics on this site are reviewed and refreshed by our editorial team on a regular schedule. They are orientation snapshots, not a live trading feed — always confirm current figures with an exchange or a dedicated data provider before acting on them.

Is the subscription free?

Yes. Our newsletter and all published articles are free to read. If we ever introduce optional premium research, core news coverage will remain freely accessible and clearly separated from any paid offering.

Can I integrate your data into my app?

We do not currently provide a public API. If you would like to reference or syndicate our editorial content, reach out through the contact page and the editorial team will follow up with you.
